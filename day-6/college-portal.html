Welcome to IGNOU

Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) is a renowned institution that offers distance education and open learning opportunities to students across India and abroad. Established in 1985, IGNOU has been at the forefront of providing quality education to learners who may not have access to traditional classroom settings.

IGNOU offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and certificate programs in various disciplines, including arts, science, commerce, management, education, and more. The university's flexible learning approach allows students to pursue their education at their own pace, making it an ideal choice for working professionals, homemakers, and individuals seeking to enhance their knowledge and skills.
